Who We Are

About CSK Security

CSK – Core Shield Keepers is a professional security and event support services company based in Pune, Maharashtra, serving clients since 2025.

We specialize in delivering disciplined, reliable, and professional security solutions for events, clubs, lounges, private gatherings, commercial spaces, and residential establishments.

Our team is committed to maintaining safety, smooth crowd flow, controlled access, and professional on-ground support — with a focus on professionalism, punctuality, and dependable service.

At CSK, we believe that trust, discipline, and strong presence are the foundation of effective protection and event management.
